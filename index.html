🚨 Rexon Report Policy

📜 Introduction

Rexon is committed to maintaining a secure and scam-free trading environment. This policy outlines what constitutes a valid or invalid report, how to file a report correctly, and the consequences for fraudulent reports.

🚨 Reports that do not meet these guidelines will be automatically denied.

✅ Valid Reports

🔹 Invite Rewards Scam

  • Scams that involve server invite rewards
  • Example: A server owner refuses to deliver a promised item after invites are completed.

🔹 Transfer Scams

  • Money transfer scams such as money doubling or guaranteed gambling.
  • Example: A user promises to increase their money by gambling.

🔹 Exchange Scams

  • Scams involving the exchange of funds (e.g., Crypto → USD)
  • Example: A user promises to transfer your USD to Crypto or vice versa.

🔹 Breach of Agreement

  • Violation of an agreed-upon service term or contract.
  • Example: A seller refuses to deliver an item after payment is made.

🔹 Nitro Scams

  • Fraudulent Discord Nitro sales or giveaways.
  • Example: A seller claims to provide Nitro but never sends it.

🔹 Giveaway Scams

  • Failure to deliver a promised giveaway prize.
  • Example: A user runs a fake giveaway and refuses to reward the winner.

🔹 Account Takeover / Attempt

  • Unauthorized access to or attempts to take control of another user’s account.
  • Example: Phishing links, credential theft, or token grabbing.

❌ Invalid Reports

🔸 Discord TOS or Legal Violations

  • Example: Selling fake IDs – a legal issue, not a scam report.

🔸 Under $1 USD

  • Example: Reporting a scam over $0.50 is not allowed.

🔸 Malware Reports

  • Should be directed to Discord Trust & Safety.

⚠️ How to File a Report

✅ Format:

  • User Info: Discord Username + ID (e.g., Scammer#0001 | 123456789012345678)
  • Details: What was bought/sold, price, currency
  • Proof: Screenshots of the agreement, payment, and messages

❌ Denied if:

  • No proof
  • No clear scam occurred
  • Misunderstanding, not fraud

🚨 Fake Report Consequences

  • 1️⃣ First offense: Temporary blacklist
  • 2️⃣ Second offense: Permanent ban from reporting

🔹 Final Disclaimer

  • We reserve the right to deny any unqualified report
  • We may request more proof
  • Repeat abuse = permanent blacklist
📌 Need help? Open a case in the support server.
