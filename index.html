🚨 Rexon Report Policy

📜 Introduction

Rexon is committed to maintaining a secure and scam-free trading environment. This policy outlines what constitutes a valid or invalid report, how to file a report correctly, and the consequences for fraudulent reports.

🚨 Reports that do not meet these guidelines will be automatically denied.

✅ Valid Reports

Reports that fall under these categories will be investigated and acted upon if verified.

🔹 Invite Rewards Scam

  • Scams that involve server invite rewards
  • Example: A server owner refuses to deliver an promised item after invites are completed.

🔹 Transfer Scams

  • Money transfer scams such as money doubling or guaranteed gambling.
  • Example: A user promises to increase their money by gambling.

🔹 Exchange Scams

  • Scams involving the exchange of funds (Ex. Crypto -> USD)
  • Example: A user promises to transfer your USD to Crypto or Crypto to USD or Crypto to Crypto.

🔹 Breach of Agreement

  • Violation of an agreed-upon service term or contract.
  • Example: A seller refuses to deliver an item after payment is made.

🔹 Nitro Scams

  • Fraudulent Discord Nitro sales, giveaways, or unauthorized charges.
  • Example: A seller claims to provide a Nitro subscription but never sends it.

🔹 Giveaway Scams

  • Failure to deliver a promised giveaway prize.
  • Example: A user runs a fake giveaway and refuses to reward the winner.

🔹 Account Takeover / Attempted Account Takeover

  • Unauthorized access to or attempts to take control of another user’s account.
  • Example: Phishing links, credential theft, or token grabbing attempts.

❌ Invalid Reports

Reports that do not fall under these categories will be automatically denied.

🔸 Violations of Discord TOS or Local Laws

  • Reports regarding TOS violations or legal issues.
  • Example: A user is selling fake IDs – this is a legal issue, not a scam report.

🔸 Disputes Under $1 USD

  • Reports must involve an amount greater than $1 USD (or equivalent).
  • Example: Reporting a scam over $0.50 is not allowed.

🔸 Malware-Related Reports

  • Reports involving viruses, ransomware, or hacking tools should be directed to Discord Trust & Safety.

⚠️ How to File a Proper Report

To ensure your report is accepted, follow this correct format:

✅ Correct Report Format:

  • User Information: Discord Username & ID (e.g., Scammer#0001 | 123456789012345678).
  • Transaction Details: Product/Service, Amount Paid, Currency Used.
  • Proof: Screenshots of agreement, payment, and scam.

❌ Invalid Reports Will Be Denied If:

  • No proof is provided.
  • No clear scam occurred.
  • The issue is a misunderstanding, not fraud.

🚨 Consequences for Fake Reports

Filing a fraudulent report is a serious offense. Users caught submitting fake claims will face:

  • 1️⃣ First Offense: Temporary blacklist from reporting.
  • 2️⃣ Second Offense: Permanent ban from reporting & possible blacklist.

🔹 Final Disclaimer

  • Rexon reserves the right to deny any report that does not meet our guidelines.
  • We may request additional proof before taking action.
  • Repeated false reports may result in a permanent blacklist.
📌 Need Help? If your report meets our policy, please open a case in Support server.
